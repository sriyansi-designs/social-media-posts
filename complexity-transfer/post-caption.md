## Alternate, naming the principle

Naming Tesler's Law gives the post something citable, which tends to travel
further with a design audience. The video's closing frame names it either way.

---

You tap one button and the booking just works.

Behind it: retries, timezones, partial refunds, five permission states.

That complexity didn't disappear. It moved.

Tangled system → carried by the team → one clean line for the user.

Tesler's Law: every product has a complexity that can't be designed away.
It can only be moved. Good design decides who carries it.
