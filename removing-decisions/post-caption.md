# LinkedIn caption

Matched to the pattern in Sriyansi's existing posts: second-person
observation, "isn't only X, it's Y" reframe, mechanism compressed with
arrows, one-line implication. ~48 words, five Title Case hashtags ending on
#BuildWithRoro. No em dashes.

## Primary

---

You open a flow to do one thing and it asks you three questions first.

Which format. Which region. Metric or imperial.

None of those were your decision → all three were unanswered work → the
product handed them over.

Every unnecessary decision is work passed to the user. A default is you
deciding once so they don't decide every time.

#ProductDesign #ProductThinking #UXDesign #ProductPsychology #BuildWithRoro

---

## Alternate, sharper on the nuance

---

Ten paths to the one thing they came to do.

Every fork looks like flexibility. Most of them are just decisions nobody on
the team wanted to make.

Three forks → three pauses → same destination.

Removing choices isn't the goal. Removing the ones that were never really
theirs is.

#ProductDesign #ProductThinking #UXDesign #ProductPsychology #BuildWithRoro
