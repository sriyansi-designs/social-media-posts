## Primary

---

You open the screen and everything is bold.

Nothing stands out, because everything does.

Ten things shouting → your eye checks all of them → it still misses the one
that matters.

Hierarchy isn't making one thing louder. It's being willing to make nine
things quieter.

## Alternate, leading with the count

---

Nine stops before your eye finds the button that matters.

That isn't a user problem. It's a hierarchy problem.

Ten elements competing → every one of them checked → the primary action lost
in the middle of it.

Attention is finite. When everything demands it, nothing gets it.
