## Primary

---

You want the product to be simple and flexible. Fast and controlled.

So you try to balance both, and the scale sits level.

Level isn't balance → level is no decision → nobody gets the thing they came
for.

Design isn't finding the balance. It's choosing the tilt, and naming what
pays for it.

#ProductDesign #ProductThinking #UXDesign #ProductPsychology #BuildWithRoro

---

## Alternate, harder edge

---

Every product you admire is deliberately unbalanced.

Someone sat in a room and decided what it was allowed to be bad at.

Simplicity up → flexibility pays. Speed up → control pays. There is no
version where nothing pays.

A trade-off you didn't choose is still a trade-off. You just let it happen to
you.

#ProductDesign #ProductThinking #UXDesign #ProductPsychology #BuildWithRoro
